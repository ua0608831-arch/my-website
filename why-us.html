Common questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by product and level of customization. Tell us the item and quantity you're considering and we'll confirm what's possible.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — export is core to what we do. We pack and document orders for international shipment.

Can you produce under my own brand?

Yes. Custom branding, labelling, and packaging to your specification is the default way we work, not an add-on.

What materials can I choose from?

Materials vary by product line — fabric weight, thread colour, leather grade, and finish are all discussed as part of your brief.

How do I request a sample?

Email your design, logo, or reference to taiyoindustry.1@gmail.com and we'll follow up on next steps.

What's the typical turnaround time?

Turnaround depends on the product and order size. Share your requirements and we'll give you a timeline specific to your order.

Can I order a mix of product categories in one shipment?

Yes — that's one of the main reasons buyers work with us. Badges, apparel, bags, and other lines can be produced and shipped together.

Do you work with buyers who don't have a finished design yet?

Yes. Send us a rough idea, reference product, or description and we can help shape it into a produceable spec before sampling.
