Taiyo Industry History

With 18 years of industry experience, Taiyo Industry has built its reputation around dependable production, consistent workmanship and long-term business relationships. Our experience gives customers confidence in every stage of the order — from understanding specifications to preparing products for professional delivery.

Modern Embroidery Expertise

We specialize in modern embroidery-machine production and hand embroidery, combining technology with skilled craftsmanship. Our capabilities extend strongly into sportswear, and our production setup supports a wide range of embroidery requirements, designs, finishes and order specifications.
